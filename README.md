# Cuplark

Nine holes of mini golf seen from above, in one HTML file, built by a single prompt in Google Antigravity (Gemini 4 Argon, high effort).

Play it: https://games.gradedprompts.com/cuplark/

`index.html` is the unedited output of one run of the prompt. Nothing was changed by hand after the run.
SHA-256: `5a67eaf2d64669113cd864cd07775acc865428eeb3ec0b8c1556c8f4c1ed0e7b`

How it plays: nine holes, one screen each, par 24. Each hole adds one idea: a bank off a corner, posts in the way, bunkers that stop the ball fast, a pond that costs a stroke and sends you back, a hill that rolls a soft putt back, a gate that opens and shuts, a tunnel to a green of its own, and a last hole with all of it. Eight strokes on a hole and the ball is picked up. A full round saves your best total and your best on each hole in your browser.

Pull back anywhere and let go to putt, with a mouse or a finger; on an upright phone the course turns to fit. On a keyboard the arrow keys aim and set the power, Space putts, P pauses and M toggles sound. The nine buttons on the title start a practice round at any hole, as does `?hole=7` at the end of the address. It needs no network at all.

The prompt that built it is being prepared for Graded Prompts: https://www.gradedprompts.com/prompts/apps
